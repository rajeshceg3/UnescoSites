Summary [ edit ] Description Darasuram, Airavatesvara Temple, Gopuram, India.jpg English: Airavatesvara Temple, a 12th century Hindu temple of Dravidian architecture, art and exquisite stone carvings. Chola dynasty was a powerful Tamil dynasty in southern India, one of the longest-ruling dynasties in the world's history. Darasuram, Tamil Nadu, India. Русский: Храм Айраватешвары. XII век.
